# Fixed target guarantees

Strengthen the implementation to meet the accepted project guarantees. Never weaken those
guarantees to fit the current code.

Keep each target separate from current evidence and remaining engineering work. Correcting an
overclaim, documenting a limitation, adding a temporary refusal, or scheduling a later release
does not close the original obligation. Keep it open until the implementation and required
acceptance evidence meet the target.

Preserve or strengthen proof contracts, accepted proof floors, required coverage, acceptance
cases, negative history and principal boundaries. A failing check requires a repair; it is not
permission to lower the acceptance bar. A proved isolated dependency does not establish the
integrated system's guarantee.

Follow the accepted roadmap and its design, independent review and release gates. Apply the
touched-module publication rule to complete semantic modules, including their dependencies.
Moving code or recording a non-claim cannot remove an original release obligation. Retain
historical evidence and record corrections explicitly.
