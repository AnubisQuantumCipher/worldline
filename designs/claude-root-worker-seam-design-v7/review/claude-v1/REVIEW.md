If instead the intent is to convert the `elif` into an `else:` (the correct fix), then the handler *is* changed, contradicting "unchanged," and the design must say so explicitly. As written the specification is ambiguous and, taken literally, suppresses a native quarantine. This keeps V6-REQ-H PARTIALLY_CLOSED and clauses 04.1/07.3/12.2 and rulings D-6a/D-9a short of design-level closure.
