## 3. W-1 v4 capability for V5-B01 and V5-B02

**Assessment:** As specified, W-1 v4 cannot yet establish V5-B01 and V5-B02 together: target-owned permissions and non-writable retained references invalidate its absorbing custody argument, and protocol, journal, setup and image checks remain internally incomplete. Its protected single-tree writer routing is a plausible V5-B02 design direction, contingent on preserving successor-frame semantics and exact installed verification; no design text or author self-check supplies that evidence.

V5-B01 needs custody from the final walk to spawn, including hard-link/rename/outside-writer possibilities and excluded generated leaves. V5-B02 forbids the frame's own prohibited outside-tree effects, including writes through an already obtained descriptor to an inode that has moved outside. Merely detecting a late error is insufficient ([FrameReview:76-82](/home/sicarii/Documents/Codex/2026-10-03/read-the-two-files-and-follow/work/claude-scratch-frame-v5/review/gpt61-v1/REVIEW.md:76), [FrameReview:84-123](/home/sicarii/Documents/Codex/2026-10-03/read-the-two-files-and-follow/work/claude-scratch-frame-v5/review/gpt61-v1/REVIEW.md:84); W1 Appendix A:2847-2910).
