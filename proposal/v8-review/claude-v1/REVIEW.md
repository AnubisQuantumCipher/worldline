## Item 2 — The c8 / SIV argument

**Pull routes (N2, v8:900-939): correct, and premise-conditional.** Applied to A1 (initial namespace, euid 1000, no capabilities) against T (uid 1000 in U8, child of the initial namespace, owner W): the uid half of `__ptrace_may_access` passes; commoncap then denies because the namespaces differ and `ns_capable(U8, CAP_SYS_PTRACE)` walks U8 (owner W ≠ A1's euid 1000, owner rule does not grant) to the initial namespace, where A1 lacks `CAP_SYS_PTRACE`. This is exactly the capability half the v7 review found missing; the correction is sound **as reasoning**, conditional on P-KSRC (the quoted upstream v7.2 commoncap/ptrace/pid/fs-proc behaviour matching the installed 7.2.6-1 build — not verifiable here and correctly named, v8:1035). The W-owned vs root-owned choice is justified by P-UCOUNT (per-user counts charged at the initial level to the namespace owner; W isolates them from root's host-shared budget), v8:1970-1973.

**Push and name routes (N1, N3): closed under the stated premises.** Inherited descriptors limited and W's zero-length control buffer truncates passed fds (MSG_CTRUNC); outward pathname sockets blocked by Landlock RESOLVE_UNIX; inward MAKE_SOCK confined to the two binds; abstract sockets scoped by the private network namespace; binder/`IORING_OP_MSG_RING`/`pidfd_getfd` covered (v8:875-898). Names: on-disk search to W/root only, binds exist only in the private-propagation view namespace, cross-mount link/rename non-success (P-XMNT), file-handle decode needs mapped owners on the whole ancestor chain which g breaks (P-FH) (v8:941-957). Internally consistent under P-LL, P-NETNS, P-PROP, P-LOCK, P-XMNT, P-FH.

**Identity-map privilege risk — contained only conditionally, and correctly flagged catastrophic.** Because U8's maps include host root (`0 0 4294967295`) and the map is the identity, a set-ID-root `exec` inside U8 would hold every capability in U8 and, through the map, DAC override over every host inode. Containment is `nosuid` on every view mount (P-NOSUID) plus no-capability (P-NOCAP). The spec prominently names these as critical with "failure requires redesign, no exception" (v8:1359-1364, v8:45, v8:0.4). This is the correct, honest treatment; it is a reliance, not an establishment.

**Something else exposed by identity maps that the N2 A1-analysis does not surface (finding, see RC4):** A1 may invoke the reviewed `sicarii`→`wlscratch` sudo rule (`writer.py`, `*` argv, `-I -B`) and so obtain a process with **euid W** in the initial namespace (v8:2.1 adversary A1; §3.2 S1 row v8:318; §5.3 entry row v8:492). A euid-W process holds `CAP_SYS_PTRACE` over every U8 by the owner rule and may `setns` into any U8. The pull closure for A1 therefore ultimately rests on writer.py (driven by attacker-controlled argv) never entering a U8 or using W's owner capabilities. The design places this in the trusted base (§2.2, v8:249-251) and as a §16 code-review item (v8:1548) and premise P-SETNS (v8:1039), but N2 presents the A1 pull denial via commoncap without surfacing the A1-as-W vector; it should be named explicitly in N2 as a load-bearing obligation, not left implicit in the general trusted-base clause.

Conclusion: the SIV analysis is correct and complete only as a premise-conditional argument. It launders nothing; it does not establish custody.

## Why NOT_ENDORSED

v8 is a substantial, honest advance over v6/v7: it fixes the specific defects the v7 review named (the missing commoncap half, the undelivered C3 bytes, the P-LL/P-XDEV errno errors, the "no atom undoes another" contradiction, the sync-boolean-after-death gap, the circular first-run qualification, the false N5 "no host write"), and 7 of 11 required changes reach CLOSED_AT_SPEC_LEVEL. But it cannot be endorsed as a design because:
1. The central obligations V5-B01, V5-B02, W1-V5-PRESERVATION and W1-V6-READ-MUTATION remain unestablished; §21 is a framework with every row OPEN (v8:1925-1929).
2. A required change, W1-V7-HOLDER-STATE, is not closed: the absorbing property is unproved for the P-IOEXIT async path, and the capture (§15.3) and stream finality (§10.6) both depend on it.
3. The entire SIV rests on ~30 NAMED_UNESTABLISHED premises, including the non-universally-dischargeable P-KSRC and the catastrophic-if-wrong P-NOSUID/P-NOCAP; none is discharged.
4. W1-V7-COMPOSED-STATE and W1-V7-EVIDENCE-RESTORE are partially closed (model/specification only).
5. Form: the §9.4 / §11.4 carried sub-ranges are not independently hash-pinned.

As the v7 review itself put it, this is a plausible custody **research direction with named premises**, not yet an endorsed, established design. v8 strengthens that direction and removes the specific errors; it does not change that status.
